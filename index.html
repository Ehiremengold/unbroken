Privacy Policy for Unbroken

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Unbroken is a puzzle game made by ehiremengold ("we", "us"). This page says what the game collects, why, and how you can delete it. If anything here is unclear, write to helloehiremen@gmail.com.

What the game collects

When you pick a game name, the game creates an account for you on our server. From then on it sends us:

  • Your game name, and a four-digit number we add to tell you apart from other players with the same name. Both are shown to every player on the leaderboards.
  • An account identifier: a random code created for your account. It is not your email address, phone number or device identifier. We never ask for an email address or a password.
  • Your results: the levels you have cleared, your best time on each, your time on each daily board, and the route you drew for each of those times. The route lets us check that a time is genuine before it goes on a leaderboard.

Like most online services, our hosting provider also keeps short-lived technical logs of the requests the game makes, which include the IP address and the time of each request. We use them only to keep the service running and secure.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, best times, streak and settings (sound, theme and reminders) are stored on your phone. Daily reminders are scheduled by the phone itself; the game does not send us anything to make them work.

What we use it for

To run the game: to show the leaderboards, to keep one account per player, and to stop false times.

To improve the game, we also count what happens in it: for example that a level was solved, a hint used, a dead end reached, a result shared, or the Plus screen opened. These counts carry a random code made on your phone, not your game name, so they are not linked to you.

If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem: what went wrong, where in the game's code, and the kind of phone and system version. Crash reports carry no game name, account details or IP address.

When you buy something, Apple or Google take the payment; we never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat, which handles purchases for us, records what was bought against a random code, so the game can restore it on a new phone.

We do not show advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your information or pass it to anyone for their own purposes.

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or payment details.

Who stores it

The information is stored for us by four companies, each acting only on our instructions: Supabase, which hosts the game's database; PostHog, which keeps the usage counts; RevenueCat, which records purchases; and Sentry, which keeps crash reports.

How long we keep it

We keep your game name, number and results until you delete your account. The hosting provider's technical logs are deleted automatically after a short period, and crash reports within 90 days.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete everything we hold about you at any time.

  • In the game: open Ranks, tap Account, then Delete account. Your name, number and every result are removed from our server straight away and cannot be recovered.
  • If you can no longer use the game on that phone: write to helloehiremen@gmail.com from any address and tell us your game name and its number (for example, nova #2256). We will delete the account and its results within 30 days.

Removing the game from your phone deletes what is stored on the phone, but not your account on the leaderboard. Use one of the two ways above for that.

Names chosen by players

Game names are written by players. If you see a name that is offensive, write to helloehiremen@gmail.com with the name and its number and we will remove it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about your account, or to correct or delete it, by writing to helloehiremen@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page and the date at the top.
